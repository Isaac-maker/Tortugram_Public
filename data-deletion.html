Data Deletion

How to delete the data associated with Tortugram, step by step.

In short

  • The developer keeps no copy of your account, chats or media on any server, so there is nothing stored with us to delete.
  • What the app stores lives on your device. You can erase it yourself in under a minute (Step 1).
  • Your Telegram account and messages are held by Telegram. You can end Tortugram's access (Step 2) or delete your Telegram account (Step 3).

What data is associated with Tortugram

Tortugram does not have user accounts of its own: you sign in with your Telegram account, and the developer keeps no copy of your data on any server. The data related to Tortugram lives in the places below. For the full explanation, see the Privacy Policy.

Step 1: Delete the data on your device

This is immediate and removes everything the app stores locally: your session, the local database, cached media and settings. You will be logged out.

  • Amazon Fire TV: Settings → Applications → Manage Installed Applications → Tortugram. Choose Clear data (erases everything), Clear cache (temporary files only) or Uninstall.
  • Android phone or tablet: Settings → Apps → Tortugram → Storage → Clear storage (or Clear data) and Clear cache. Then uninstall the app if you wish.
  • Only cached media: use the app's cache management feature.

Menu names vary slightly between devices and system versions (Fire OS, Android, LineageOS and others). Uninstalling the app also removes its private storage.

Step 2: End Tortugram's access to your Telegram account

Removing the app from your device does not automatically close its session on Telegram's side. To close it, for example if you lost the device or cannot reach it:

  • Open the official Telegram app.
  • Go to Settings → Devices.
  • Tap the session that belongs to Tortugram (or any session you don't recognise) and choose Terminate session.

Never send passwords, Telegram login codes or session data. We will never ask for them.

Step 3: Delete your Telegram account or messages

Your account and messages are stored by Telegram, not by Tortugram, so Telegram is the one that can delete them. You can delete your account at my.telegram.org, or set an automatic deletion period in Telegram → Settings → Privacy & Security → Delete my account. To delete individual messages or chats, use the official Telegram app. For data requests to Telegram, see telegram.org/privacy.

Deleting a Telegram account is permanent and affects all your Telegram apps, not just Tortugram.

Your rights under privacy laws

You may exercise these rights under the data protection laws that apply in your country (for example, the right to erasure). Details are in Section 10 of the Privacy Policy. You may also ask an authorised representative to act for you.
