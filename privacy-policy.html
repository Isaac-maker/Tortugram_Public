In short

  • Tortugram is an unofficial, open-source Telegram client. It is not made, endorsed or supported by Telegram.
  • The developer runs no servers for the app. We do not collect, store, sell, rent or share your personal data.
  • Your account, chats and media travel directly between your device and Telegram through Telegram's TDLib library.
  • Tortugram has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Whatever the app keeps on your device stays in its private storage, and you can erase it at any time. See the Data Deletion page.

1Who we are and scope

Tortugram (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a free, open-source multimedia client for Telegram, designed for Amazon Fire TV and other Android devices. It is developed and published by an independent developer, Isaac-maker (GitHub: @Isaac-maker), based in Costa Rica.

This policy covers (a) the Tortugram app for Android and Fire TV and (b) this website. It does not cover Telegram, Amazon, GitHub or any other third-party service; each has its own policy.

Tortugram is an unofficial application. It uses the Telegram API and the TDLib library, but it is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Telegram FZ-LLC or Telegram Messenger Inc.

2Information the app handles

To work, the app talks to Telegram on your behalf. This is what is involved and where it goes:

  • Login data. You sign in by scanning a QR code with the official Telegram app and, if your account uses two-step verification, by entering your password. This is passed to TDLib, which sends it directly to Telegram to authenticate you. It is never sent to the developer or to any other server.
  • Chats and media. Your chat list, folders, channels, groups, and the videos, images, GIFs and music inside them are retrieved from Telegram to your device so you can browse and play them. The developer never receives or sees this content.
  • Local storage and cache. TDLib keeps a local database, your session and temporary files (such as thumbnails and partially downloaded media) in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. It is erased when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.
  • Local streaming. To play videos without downloading the whole file, the app runs a small streaming component on your device that passes data from TDLib to the built-in player. It does not send content to any server operated by the developer.
  • Preferences. Simple settings, such as the interface language, are stored on your device only.

3Information we do not collect

Tortugram does not collect, or transmit to the developer: your name, phone number or email address; your contacts; your location; the camera or microphone; files or photos outside the app; device identifiers or advertising IDs; usage analytics; or crash reports.

The app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs and no tracking technologies.

4This website

The website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not use cookies or analytics. Your browser stores your language choice (EN/ES/PT/FR/RU/ZH/HI/JA) in local storage on your own device; it is not sent to us.

When a page loads, your browser also contacts GitHub (hosting) and Google Fonts (typography). These providers may receive technical data such as your IP address and browser details, under their own privacy policies. Download buttons open GitHub Releases.

5How and why data is used

On your device, data is processed only to provide the features you ask for: browsing and playing your Telegram media. Because the developer receives no personal data from the app, there is generally no personal data for us to process.

If something is not to your liking or you have an improvement in mind, you can add it in the issues of the repository: github.com/Isaac-maker/Tortugram_Public/issues.

6Sharing and third parties

We do not sell, rent or share personal data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling. These third-party services are involved when you use Tortugram:

  • Telegram provides the messaging service. Everything you do through Tortugram is also subject to Telegram's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy: telegram.org/privacy.
  • GitHub hosts this website, the source code and the APK downloads. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces used on this website only, not in the app.
  • Amazon: if you install Tortugram from the Amazon Appstore, Amazon handles your download and account under its own Privacy Notice (amazon.com/privacy). The app itself does not access your Amazon account.

We may disclose information if the law requires it, but because we hold no user data there is normally nothing to disclose.

7Data retention

  • Developer side: we keep no user data.
  • On your device: data stays until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. See the Data Deletion page for step-by-step instructions.
  • On Telegram: retention on Telegram's servers is governed by Telegram.

8Security

Communication between the app and Telegram uses Telegram's MTProto protocol, implemented by TDLib. Local data is kept in the app's private storage, protected by the Android security sandbox. The source code is public, so anyone can inspect how data is handled.

No system is perfectly secure. Keep your device updated, and end any session you don't recognise from Telegram → Settings → Devices.

9Children

Tortugram is not directed at children under 13, and it requires a Telegram account, which is subject to Telegram's own minimum age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. In the EU/EEA and UK, the age of digital consent can be as high as 16 depending on the country. If a minor uses the app, they should do so under the supervision of a parent or adult guardian.

10Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the rights below. Because we hold no user data, our answer will usually be that we hold nothing about you. For data held by Telegram, you can exercise your rights with Telegram.

  • EU/EEA and UK (GDPR / UK GDPR): access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, withdrawal of consent, and the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.
  • California and other US states (CCPA/CPRA and similar laws): to know, delete and correct personal information, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We do not sell or share personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of, and we do not discriminate against anyone for exercising their rights.
  • Brazil (LGPD): confirmation of processing, access, correction, anonymisation or deletion, portability, information about sharing, and revocation of consent, with the right to complain to the ANPD.
  • Costa Rica (Law No. 8968): access, rectification, deletion and revocation of consent, with the right to complain to the Agency for the Protection of Individuals' Data (PRODHAB).
  • Elsewhere (for example Canada, Japan, India, Australia): we honour the equivalent rights your local law gives you.

To exercise a right, follow the steps on the Data Deletion page. If you have an improvement or suggestion, add it in the issues of the repository: github.com/Isaac-maker/Tortugram_Public/issues.

11International transfers

The developer does not transfer personal data because we do not collect it. Data flows between your device and Telegram's global infrastructure under Telegram's policy. Visits to this website are served by GitHub (United States) under the GitHub Privacy Statement and its transfer safeguards.

12Open source and transparency

Tortugram's source code is public at github.com/Isaac-maker/Tortugram_Public. You can inspect what the app does. If you find a privacy or security problem, report it in the issues section: github.com/Isaac-maker/Tortugram_Public/issues.

13Third-party content

Tortugram only shows content that your own Telegram account can already access. We do not host, upload, moderate or control that content and are not responsible for it. Report illegal content to Telegram.

14Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows the latest version, and the version published at this address is always the current one. For material changes we will post a notice on this website and in the release notes.

15Errors and improvements

If you find errors or have ideas for improvements, write to us in the issues section: github.com/Isaac-maker/Tortugram_Public/issues.

Never send passwords, Telegram login codes or session data. We will never ask for them. GitHub issues are public, so do not include personal information in them.

16Legal terms and updates

All the information in this policy is based on the legal terms that apply in your country and may be subject to change. Stay alert for updates.
